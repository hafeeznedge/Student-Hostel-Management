Hostel Frequently Asked Questions

How are rooms allocated to students?
Rooms are allocated based on availability, student preferences, and hostel policies. Allocation is managed through the Room Allocation system to ensure transparency.
What are the hostel fee payment options?
Students can pay hostel fees monthly, quarterly, or annually. Payments can be made through approved online or offline methods.
How can I submit a maintenance complaint?
Complaints can be submitted through the Complaint Management page. Once submitted, the issue is tracked until it is resolved by the hostel administration.
Are visitors allowed in the hostel?
Yes, visitors are allowed during designated visiting hours. All visitors must be registered in the Visitor Entry Log for security purposes.
What facilities are provided in the hostel?
Facilities typically include furnished rooms, Wi-Fi, electricity, water supply, security services, housekeeping, and common recreational areas.
What happens if hostel fees are delayed?
Delayed payments may result in late fees or temporary restrictions until the outstanding balance is cleared.
Can I request a room change?
Yes, room change requests can be submitted to the hostel administration and will be considered based on availability and valid reasons.
How is hostel security maintained?
Security is maintained through visitor logs, surveillance systems, entry monitoring, and on-site security staff.
